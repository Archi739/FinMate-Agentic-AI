Company: ICICI Bank Limited
Sector: Banking / Financial Services

Overview: ICICI Bank Limited is a private-sector bank headquartered in Mumbai, India. It provides banking and financial services to retail customers, businesses, corporations and institutions through physical branches and digital channels.

Business segments: ICICI Bank operates across retail banking, business banking, corporate and institutional banking, rural and inclusive banking, treasury and other financial services. Its wider group also provides insurance, asset management and other financial products.

Key financials (FY2026): Standalone net interest income and non-interest income together were ₹1,17,635 crore. Core operating profit was ₹70,401 crore and profit after tax was ₹50,147 crore. Consolidated profit after tax was ₹54,208 crore.

Strengths:
- Diversified retail, business and corporate banking operations.
- Large digital banking platform and growing customer base.
- Strong capital position and improving asset-quality indicators.

Risks:
- Credit risk can affect provisions and profitability if borrower defaults increase.
- Changes in interest rates, liquidity conditions and market conditions can affect margins and treasury income.
- Cybersecurity, technology and operational risks are important because of the bank's extensive digital operations.

Recent developments:
- ICICI Bank's FY2026 standalone profit after tax increased 6.2% to ₹50,147 crore.
- Total loan portfolio grew 15.8% year-on-year to ₹15,53,893 crore at March 31, 2026.
- The bank continued investing in digital capabilities and expanded selected businesses within its financial-services group.

Source: ICICI Bank Integrated Annual Report 2025-26, Financial Performance, Business Overview and Risk Management sections.
