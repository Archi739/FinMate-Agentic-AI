Company: Maruti Suzuki India Limited
Sector: Automotive

Overview: Maruti Suzuki India Limited is India's major passenger-vehicle manufacturer and is headquartered in New Delhi. The company manufactures and sells passenger vehicles, components and spare parts and has a large domestic sales and service network.

Business segments: Maruti Suzuki primarily operates as a single automotive segment covering the manufacture, purchase and sale of motor vehicles, components and spare parts. Its product portfolio includes hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, MPVs and vehicles using petrol, CNG and electric powertrains.

Key financials (FY2026): Total revenue from operations was ₹1,83,316 crore. Net profit was ₹14,445.4 crore. Basic earnings per share was ₹459.46. Total annual sales were 24,22,713 vehicles, including 19,74,939 domestic sales and 4,47,774 exports.

Strengths:
- Large domestic passenger-vehicle market presence and extensive distribution network.
- Strong manufacturing scale and established supplier ecosystem.
- Record exports and increasing product diversification across SUVs and multiple powertrains.

Risks:
- Automobile demand can be affected by economic conditions, consumer confidence and financing costs.
- Commodity prices, foreign-exchange movements and supply-chain disruptions can affect manufacturing costs.
- Increasing competition in SUVs, electric vehicles and other vehicle categories requires continuous product and technology investment.

Recent developments:
- Maruti Suzuki recorded its highest-ever annual sales of 24.22 lakh vehicles in FY2026.
- Exports reached a record 4.47 lakh vehicles, making the company India's top passenger-vehicle exporter for the fifth consecutive year.
- The company continued expanding its SUV portfolio and introduced its first battery-electric vehicle, the e VITARA, for international markets.

Source: Maruti Suzuki Annual Integrated Report 2025-26 and FY2025-26 Financial Results.
