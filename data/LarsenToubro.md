Company: Larsen & Toubro Limited (L&T)
Sector: Engineering, Construction and Technology

Overview: Larsen & Toubro Limited is an Indian engineering and construction company with businesses spanning infrastructure, energy, defence, technology and other engineering-related activities. It executes large projects in India and international markets.

Business segments: L&T's major business areas include Infrastructure, Energy, Hi-Tech Manufacturing, IT & Technology Services and Financial Services. Its infrastructure activities cover buildings, transportation, water, power transmission and other major projects.

Key financials (FY2026): Consolidated gross revenue from operations was ₹2,85,874 crore. Profit for the year was ₹18,954 crore and profit attributable to owners including exceptional items was ₹16,084 crore. Consolidated basic EPS was ₹116.93.

Strengths:
- Large engineering and project-execution capabilities across multiple sectors.
- Diversified order book across infrastructure, energy, manufacturing and technology.
- Strong presence in large domestic and international projects.

Risks:
- Large projects can face execution delays, cost overruns and changes in project scope.
- Commodity-price movements, interest rates and foreign-exchange movements can affect project economics.
- Geopolitical conditions and disruptions to global supply chains can affect international operations.

Recent developments:
- L&T reported consolidated gross revenue of ₹2,85,874 crore in FY2026.
- The company continued expanding its infrastructure, energy, defence and technology businesses.
- L&T's consolidated profit attributable to owners including exceptional items was ₹16,084 crore.

Source: Larsen & Toubro Integrated Annual Report 2025-26, Overall Financial Review, Management Discussion & Analysis and Risk Management sections.
