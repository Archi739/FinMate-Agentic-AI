Company: HDFC Bank Limited
Sector: Banking / Financial Services

Overview: HDFC Bank Limited is a private-sector bank headquartered in Mumbai, India. It provides banking and financial services to retail customers, businesses, institutions and corporate clients through a large branch and digital network.

Business segments: HDFC Bank's principal business segments are Retail, Wholesale and Treasury. Retail banking serves individuals and small businesses through deposits, loans, payments and other financial products. Wholesale banking serves corporations, institutions and government-related entities. Treasury manages the bank's balance sheet, liquidity, investments and market-related activities.

Key financials (FY2026): Net revenues were ₹1,91,218.60 crore. Profit after tax was ₹74,671.30 crore. Return on Average Assets was 1.94%, Return on Equity was 14.3%, and the cost-to-income ratio was 38.0%.

Strengths:
- Large deposit and advances base supported by an extensive physical and digital distribution network.
- Diversified retail, wholesale and treasury businesses provide multiple sources of banking income.
- Strong digital transaction adoption, with 98% of total financial transactions conducted digitally during FY2026.

Risks:
- Credit risk from borrowers can affect asset quality and profitability.
- Liquidity and market risks can arise from changes in interest rates, market conditions and funding requirements.
- Technology, cybersecurity and data risks are important given the bank's large-scale digital operations.

Recent developments:
- Total deposits reached ₹31,05,250.5 crore and total advances reached ₹29,37,166.3 crore during FY2026.
- The bank continued investing in technology, digital banking, payments and analytics.
- HDFC Bank continued strengthening its retail, mortgage, government and institutional, wealth-management and corporate businesses.

Source: HDFC Bank Integrated Annual Report 2025-26, Business Segments, Financial Performance and Risk Management sections.
