Company: Infosys Limited
Sector: Information Technology / IT Services

Overview: Infosys Limited is a global technology services and consulting company headquartered in Bengaluru, India. It provides digital services, consulting, technology solutions and business-process services to enterprises across multiple industries and geographies.

Business segments: Infosys serves clients through industry sectors including Financial Services, Retail, Communication, Energy/Utilities/Resources, Manufacturing, Life Sciences and Healthcare, and Hi-Tech. Its service portfolio includes digital transformation, cloud, data and analytics, engineering, consulting and application services.

Key financials (FY2026): Use the revenue, net profit, operating margin and diluted EPS reported in the Infosys FY2025-26 Annual Report.

Strengths:
- Strong global delivery capabilities and a large technology workforce.
- Broad presence across industries and geographic markets.
- Significant capabilities in cloud, AI, digital transformation and consulting services.

Risks:
- Exposure to changes in technology spending and economic conditions in major client markets.
- Competition for skilled technology professionals can affect employee retention and costs.
- Rapid technological changes, including generative AI, can alter demand for traditional technology services.

Recent developments:
- Infosys continued expanding its AI, cloud and digital transformation capabilities.
- The company continued investing in employee reskilling and new technology offerings.
- Infosys continued working with global clients on digital transformation and AI-led initiatives.
