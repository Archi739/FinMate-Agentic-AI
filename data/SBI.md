Company: State Bank of India (SBI)
Sector: Banking / Financial Services

Overview: State Bank of India is India's largest public-sector bank and provides banking and financial services to individuals, businesses, institutions and government entities. It has a large domestic branch network and an international presence.

Business segments: SBI's major businesses include Retail Banking, Corporate Banking, International Banking, Treasury, and other financial services. Retail Banking serves individuals and small businesses, while Corporate Banking provides financing and transaction services to large companies and institutions.

Key financials (FY2026): Profit after tax was ₹80,032 crore. Operating profit was ₹1,23,015 crore. Earnings per share was ₹87.59. Return on assets was 1.12% and return on equity was 18.57%.

Strengths:
- Very large customer, deposit and branch network across India.
- Diversified banking operations across retail, corporate, international and treasury businesses.
- Strong digital banking capabilities and continued investment in technology.

Risks:
- Credit risk from borrowers can affect asset quality and profitability.
- Changes in interest rates and liquidity conditions can affect margins and treasury operations.
- Cybersecurity, technology and operational risks remain important for a large digital banking network.

Recent developments:
- SBI's FY2026 net profit increased to ₹80,032 crore.
- The bank continued its Digital First, Customer First strategy and expanded digital banking capabilities.
- Asset quality improved, with the gross NPA ratio at 1.49% and net NPA ratio at 0.39% at March 31, 2026.

Source: State Bank of India Annual Report 2025-26, Financial Performance, Business Overview and Risk Management sections.
