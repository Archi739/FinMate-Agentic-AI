Company: Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited (formerly Tata Motors Limited)
Sector: Automotive

Overview: Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited is an Indian automotive company with passenger vehicles, electric vehicles and Jaguar Land Rover operations. During FY2026, the Tata Motors group completed a major restructuring and demerger of its commercial-vehicle and passenger-vehicle businesses.

Business segments: The passenger-vehicle business includes Tata passenger cars and SUVs and Tata.ev electric vehicles. The consolidated passenger-vehicle company also includes Jaguar Land Rover, which operates the Jaguar and Land Rover luxury-vehicle businesses.

Key financials (FY2026): Consolidated revenue from continuing operations was ₹3,35,582 crore. Consolidated EBITDA margin was 6.8%. Profit before tax before exceptional items was ₹2,519 crore. Profit after tax from continuing operations was a loss of ₹1,377 crore and basic EPS from continuing operations was negative ₹4.43.

Strengths:
- Strong portfolio of passenger vehicles and SUVs in the Indian market.
- Established electric-vehicle portfolio and growing EV capabilities.
- Global luxury-vehicle presence through Jaguar Land Rover.

Risks:
- Automotive demand is sensitive to economic conditions, interest rates and consumer spending.
- Commodity prices, supply-chain disruptions and foreign-exchange movements can affect profitability.
- Jaguar Land Rover faces exposure to geopolitical conditions, tariffs, technology transitions and changing demand in major markets.

Recent developments:
- Tata Motors completed the restructuring and demerger of its commercial-vehicle and passenger-vehicle businesses during FY2026.
- Tata.ev continued expanding its electric-vehicle portfolio, with 92,179 EV wholesales reported for FY2026.
- FY2026 profitability was affected by challenges at Jaguar Land Rover, including a cyber incident, tariffs and other operating pressures.

Source: Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Integrated Annual Report 2025-26, Financial Highlights, Business Review and Risk Management sections.
