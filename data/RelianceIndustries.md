Company: Reliance Industries Limited
Sector: Conglomerate / Energy, Retail and Digital Services

Overview: Reliance Industries Limited (RIL) is a diversified Indian enterprise with businesses spanning energy, materials, retail, digital services and media and entertainment. Its businesses serve large consumer and industrial markets in India and internationally.

Business segments: RIL's principal operating and reporting segments are Oil to Chemicals (O2C), Oil and Gas, Retail and Digital Services. The group also has significant Media and Entertainment activities and other businesses, including new energy initiatives.

Key financials (FY2026): Consolidated revenue was ₹11,75,919 crore. Profit after tax attributable to the company and associates/joint ventures was ₹95,754 crore. EBITDA was ₹2,07,911 crore and EBITDA margin was 17.7%. Earnings per share was ₹59.69.

Strengths:
- Highly diversified business portfolio across energy, consumer and digital markets.
- Strong scale in India's retail and digital-services markets, supported by large customer and store networks.
- Significant cash generation and continued investment across growth businesses.

Risks:
- O2C performance is exposed to volatility in crude oil prices, refining margins, petrochemical cycles and geopolitical disruptions.
- Oil and Gas production can decline as fields mature, while changes in gas prices can affect profitability.
- Large capital requirements across growth businesses increase the importance of disciplined capital allocation and execution.

Recent developments:
- Reliance reported record FY2026 consolidated revenue of ₹11,75,919 crore and PAT of ₹95,754 crore.
- Digital Services, Retail and Media & Entertainment recorded strong growth during FY2026.
- Capital expenditure increased to ₹1,44,271 crore, with investments directed toward O2C, New Energy, Digital Services and Retail.

Source: Reliance Industries Limited Integrated Annual Report 2025-26, Financial Performance and Review, Segment Information and Risk Management sections.
