Company: Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)
Sector: Information Technology / IT Services

Overview: Tata Consultancy Services Limited is an Indian IT services, consulting and business solutions company. It provides technology and digital transformation services to enterprises globally and operates across North America, Latin America, the United Kingdom, Continental Europe, Asia Pacific, India and the Middle East and Africa.

Business segments: TCS uses industry verticals as its primary go-to-market business segments. The major vertical clusters are Banking, Financial Services and Insurance (BFSI), Communication, Media and Technology (CMT), Consumer Business, Life Sciences and Healthcare, Manufacturing, and Others including Energy, Resources and Utilities, Public Services and Products.

Key financials (FY2026): Revenue from operations was ₹2,67,021 crore. Profit after tax was ₹52,820 crore. Operating income was ₹66,838 crore and the reported operating margin was approximately 25%. Earnings per share was ₹145.99.

Strengths:
- Broad geographic presence and a diversified industry portfolio reduce dependence on a single market or vertical.
- Large-scale technology capabilities, strong customer relationships and continued investment in AI and employee reskilling support its transformation strategy.
- Strong financial performance and cash generation provide resources for investments and shareholder returns.

Risks:
- Competition for specialised technology and AI talent can make attracting and retaining skilled employees more difficult.
- Rapid advances in foundational AI models may change traditional IT-service and pricing models and may cause customers to reconsider existing technology spending.
- Cybersecurity, data privacy and intellectual-property risks remain important operational and business risks for a global technology-services company.

Recent developments:
- TCS continued its strategy of becoming an AI-led technology services company and increased investment in AI capabilities and workforce skills.
- During FY2026, TCS acquired Coastal Cloud and List Engage and established HyperVault as part of its Build–Partner–Acquire approach.
- TCS reported FY2026 annual revenue of about $30 billion and an operating margin of 25%.

Source: TCS Integrated Annual Report 2025-26, FY2026, Business Overview and Management Discussion and Analysis/Risk Management sections.
