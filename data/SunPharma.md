Company: Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Limited
Sector: Pharmaceuticals

Overview: Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Limited is an Indian pharmaceutical company that develops, manufactures and markets medicines in India and international markets. Its portfolio includes generic medicines, specialty medicines, innovative medicines and active pharmaceutical ingredients.

Business segments: Sun Pharma operates across India, the United States, emerging markets and other developed markets. Its businesses include generic medicines, innovative medicines, specialty products, APIs and other pharmaceutical activities.

Key financials (FY2026): Consolidated sales were ₹58,220.1 crore. EBITDA was ₹17,731.4 crore and the EBITDA margin was 30.3%. Net profit was ₹11,479.4 crore. Research and development investment was ₹3,554 crore.

Strengths:
- Large global pharmaceutical presence with a diversified geographic portfolio.
- Strong position in specialty and innovative medicines alongside generics.
- Significant investment in research and development supports new product development.

Risks:
- Pharmaceutical companies face regulatory, product-quality and compliance risks across different markets.
- Patent challenges, pricing pressure and generic competition can affect revenues and margins.
- Clinical-development and product-launch outcomes can be uncertain and require substantial investment.

Recent developments:
- Sun Pharma reported FY2026 sales growth of 11.9%.
- Global Innovative Medicines sales reached US$1.42 billion and represented 20.7% of sales excluding milestones.
- The company continued investing in research and development and expanding its innovative-medicines portfolio.

Source: Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Limited Annual Report 2025-26 and FY2025-26 Consolidated Financial Results.
