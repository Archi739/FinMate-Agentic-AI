Company: Hindustan Unilever Limited (HUL)
Sector: Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG)

Overview: Hindustan Unilever Limited is a major Indian FMCG company and part of the Unilever Group. It sells consumer products across home care, beauty and wellbeing, personal care and foods through a large portfolio of brands and distribution network.

Business segments: HUL's continuing operations are organised around Home Care, Beauty & Wellbeing, Personal Care and Foods. These businesses cover products such as fabric and home cleaning, skin and hair care, personal hygiene, nutrition and packaged foods.

Key financials (FY2026): Turnover was ₹63,763 crore. EBITDA was ₹15,054 crore and the EBITDA margin was 23.6%. Profit after tax was ₹10,652 crore and earnings per share was ₹45.25.

Strengths:
- Large portfolio of established consumer brands.
- Extensive distribution and retail reach across urban and rural India.
- Scale in sourcing, manufacturing, marketing and innovation supports its consumer-goods operations.

Risks:
- Changes in commodity prices can affect input costs and margins.
- Consumer demand can be affected by inflation, economic conditions and changes in disposable income.
- Competition from established FMCG companies and emerging brands can affect market share and pricing.

Recent developments:
- HUL reported 5% underlying sales growth and 4% underlying volume growth in FY2026.
- The company continued portfolio rotation, brand investment and innovation across its categories.
- HUL continued focusing on stronger execution and growth across its brands and channels.
