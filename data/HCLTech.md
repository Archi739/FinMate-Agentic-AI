Company: HCLTech
Sector: Information Technology / IT Services

Overview: HCLTech is a global technology company headquartered in Noida, India. It provides technology services and products focused on AI, digital transformation, engineering, cloud and software, serving enterprises across major industries and geographies.

Business segments: HCLTech operates through three major segments: IT and Business Services, which provides technology and business solutions; Engineering and R&D Services, which provides engineering and product-development capabilities; and HCLSoftware, which provides enterprise software products.

Key financials (FY2026): Revenue from operations was ₹1,30,144 crore. Net income was ₹17,361 crore. EBIT was ₹22,397 crore, representing an EBIT margin of 17.2%. Diluted EPS was ₹64.01.

Strengths:
- Broad capabilities across AI, digital, engineering, cloud and software.
- Diversified presence across industries and global markets.
- Strong deal pipeline, with total contract value of new deal wins reaching $9.3 billion in FY2026.

Risks:
- Global enterprise technology spending can be affected by economic uncertainty and reduced discretionary technology budgets.
- Rapid advances in AI can disrupt existing technology-service models and require continued investment in new capabilities.
- Geopolitical uncertainty and changes in global operating conditions can affect international business operations.

Recent developments:
- HCLTech reported annualized Advanced AI revenue of $620 million in FY2026.
- Services revenue reached ₹1,18,158 crore, while HCLSoftware revenue was ₹11,986 crore.
- HCLTech continued expanding its AI-led offerings and technology capabilities during FY2026.

Source: HCLTech Integrated Annual Report 2025-26, Business Overview, Management Discussion and Analysis and Risk Management sections.
